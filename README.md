# Privacy Policy for ZH VPN

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

ZH VPN ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our VPN application handles your information.

## 1. No-Logs Policy
We strictly follow a **No-Logs Policy**. We do NOT monitor, record, log, store, or share your browsing activities, DNS queries, connection timestamps, traffic data, or IP addresses while using our VPN service.

## 2. Permissions We Use
- **Android VpnService:** Required to create a secure, encrypted WireGuard/V2Ray tunnel to protect your internet traffic and privacy.
- **Camera Permission (Optional):** Used solely for scanning WireGuard/VPN QR codes when you choose to add a custom server via QR code. No images or camera data are stored or transmitted.
- **Query All Packages (Split Tunneling):** Used locally on your device to allow you to select which apps should use or bypass the VPN tunnel.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
Our app may display third-party promotional links or advertisements to support free server maintenance. Clicking on external links may direct you to third-party websites governed by their own privacy policies.

## 4. Data Security
We use industry-standard WireGuard and cryptographic protocols to secure your network connection against unauthorized access.
